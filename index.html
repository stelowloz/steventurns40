    26 nights, 12 places to sleep

    Where to stay, and where to stay for less

    Nothing is booked yet. "Our pick" is where we're aiming: nice, not five-star-only. Most picks run roughly $120 to $330 a night in June. The ryokan (dinner and breakfast included) and the in-park Disney hotels cost more. The cheaper column is close enough to meet us for breakfast.

    Six ways to do this for less

    1. Join one leg, not four. A single round trip to Tokyo covers Pride weekend and the birthday.
    2. Sleep in the cheaper column. We'll all be at the same parade, park, and dinner anyway.
    3. Do one Tokyo park instead of two. If you pick one, pick DisneySea on June 8.
    4. Book an inside stateroom. Same ship, same shows, same birthday cake for Patrick.
    5. Eat where the list says. The best meals on this trip are market stalls and hawker centres that cost a few dollars.
    6. Book refundable rooms first. Lock a cancellable rate now, then rebook if prices drop.
